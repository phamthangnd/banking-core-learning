# Phase 13 — Microservices Evolution

## Objective
Understand when and how to extract services from the modular monolith.

## Candidates
- Auth
- Customer
- Account
- Transaction
- Notification
- Reporting

## Exercises
- define bounded contexts
- identify data ownership
- introduce API/event contracts
- extract one service
- use Kafka for integration
- handle eventual consistency

## Rule
Do not split services merely because it is fashionable.
Document the reason, trade-offs, operational cost and consistency implications.
